Our Services

End-to-end sourcing — from finding the right factory to delivering goods to your door in Pakistan. No middlemen, no guesswork.

🏭
Service 01
Supplier Sourcing & Verification
We find and rigorously vet Chinese manufacturers so you never deal with scam suppliers or unverified factories again.
  • Shortlist best-matched factories within 48 hours
  • On-site or video audits of business licences & capacity
  • Certification & compliance checks before you commit
🤝
Service 02
Product Sourcing & Price Negotiation
Our China-based Mandarin-speaking team secures the lowest factory prices, flexible MOQs, and favorable payment terms on your behalf.
  • Competitive multi-supplier quote comparison
  • Native Mandarin negotiators — no language barriers
  • MOQ flexibility for small first orders
🔬
Service 03
Quality Inspection & Control
We catch defects before goods leave the factory — not after they reach your warehouse. Every batch is inspected with full photo and video reports.
  • Sample review & approval before mass production
  • Pre-shipment QC inspection against your specs
  • Detailed photo/video report sent to you before dispatch
🚢
Service 04
Logistics & Shipping Management
Sea freight, air cargo, or express courier — we handle everything end-to-end including customs clearance at Karachi, Lahore Dry Port, and Islamabad.
  • Door-to-door delivery anywhere in Pakistan
  • Real-time tracking with dedicated WhatsApp support
  • Full documentation: invoice, COO, HS codes, clearance
📦
Service 05
Private Label & Product Customization
Launch your own brand with factory-direct private label manufacturing — custom packaging, logos, colors, and product specs tailored to your market.
  • Custom boxes, pouches, labels & branding
  • OEM/ODM product modifications to your specs
  • Full product line development from concept to delivery
